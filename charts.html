Aortic diameter by height

Growth-chart style curves for each level of the aorta, with the z-score bands used by the calculator. Add a child's visits to follow them over time.

Curves show the median and z = −2, +2, +3.5 and +5 for a child of typical build (height-and-sex model). Shading follows the calculator's bands. Hover the chart to read values at any height.

Example visits are shown: a boy followed from 6 to 10 years. Edit them, clear them, or add your own. Entries stay in this browser only.
